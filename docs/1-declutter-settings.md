# Step 1 – Turn off Samsung's ads and tracking (no tools needed)

Do this first. It takes about ten minutes, needs only the remote, and removes
most of the ads, autoplaying previews and data collection.

Samsung renames these menus almost every year, so the exact path on your TV may
differ a little. If a path doesn't match, open **Settings** and use the
search icon to find the words in **bold**.

## Privacy and ad tracking

Settings → All Settings → **General & Privacy** → **Terms & Privacy**
(older models: Settings → Support → Terms & Policy)

Turn these **off** or click **Disagree** on each one:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Viewing Information Services** | ACR: identifies what's on screen, including HDMI devices, and uses it for ads |
| **Interest-Based Advertisement** | Personalised ads on the home screen |
| **Voice Recognition Services** | Sends voice data to Samsung (you lose voice control) |
| **Privacy Choices / Marketing** | Marketing messages and data sharing |

Also look for **Reset PSID** (Personalised Service ID) in the same place and
use it to reset your advertising ID once.

## Stop the TV opening into Samsung's own content

Settings → All Settings → General & Privacy → **Start Screen Option** (also
called **Smart Features** on some models):

- **Autorun Smart Hub** → off. Stops the home bar/screen opening on its own at power-on.
- **Start with Samsung TV Plus** / **Autorun Samsung TV Plus** → off.
- **Autorun Last App** → **on**. The TV then reopens the last app you used.
  Once NIfty is installed (Step 3), if NIfty is the last thing you had open,
  the TV will start up in NIfty. Not every model has this option.
- **Autoplay** / **Content preview** / **Show Universal Guide at start** → off.

## Fewer notifications and promotions

- Settings → General & Privacy → **Notifications**: turn off notifications and
  **Auto Show** for promotions and new content.
- Settings → Support → **Software Update** → leave **Auto Update** however you prefer.
  Updates sometimes switch ad settings back on. Check this page again after
  each big update.

## Remove apps you don't use

Home → **Apps** → ⚙ (Settings/gear) → pick an app → **Delete**. Some
built-in apps (e.g. Samsung TV Plus on many models) can't be deleted, but
NIfty hides them from your home screen.

## Don't connect the TV to the internet at all? (optional)

If you only watch through an Apple TV / Shield / Roku / Fire TV box plugged in
over HDMI, the most private option is to **forget the Wi-Fi network** and leave
the TV offline. Everything above becomes irrelevant. NIfty (Step 3) only makes
sense if you use the TV's own apps.
